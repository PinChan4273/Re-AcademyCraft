![title]
Imag Fusor

![brief]
Refine Imag Crystals by combining IF with Imag Phase Liquid.

![content]
The unstable structure of a raw Imag Crystal limits how much energy it can store. An __Imag Fusor__ can refine it into higher-purity forms.

Insert a Low-Purity Imag Crystal to produce a Mid-Purity Imag Crystal. This recipe consumes __3,000 mB__ of Imag Phase Liquid.

Insert a Mid-Purity Imag Crystal to produce a High-Purity Imag Crystal. This recipe consumes __8,000 mB__ of Imag Phase Liquid—the machine's full tank.

The fusor also consumes IF while processing. Filled Matter Units can supply 1,000 mB each and are returned as empty units. Make sure the output slot, liquid tank, and energy supply are ready before starting.
