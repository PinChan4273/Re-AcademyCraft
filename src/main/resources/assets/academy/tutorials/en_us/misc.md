# Toast Cat

__Toast Cat__ is available only in Creative Mode.

Murphy's law says toast lands butter-side down, while a cat is said to land on its feet. Attach the toast to the cat and you have the Academy City answer to perpetual motion.

Right-click it to connect to a randomly selected Wireless Node whose range covers it. Right-click it again to disconnect. A message reports the selected node or explains why no connection was made.

# Ability Interferer

The __Ability Interferer__ prevents non-creative survival players inside its configured range from using abilities while the machine is switched on and has enough IF.

Use its panel to switch it on or off and set a range from 10 to 100 blocks. Its energy cost increases with the square of the range and is charged every ten ticks. The panel stores a whitelist, but the current runtime does not exempt those names; do not rely on the whitelist for protection.

# Commands

The __/aim__ command can inspect or modify your own ability data while you are in Creative Mode or after an operator enables its cheats with __/aim cheats_on__. The operator-only __/aimp <player>__ command edits another player's data.

Use Minecraft's command suggestions and Tab completion to see the available subcommands and arguments. The legacy __/aim help__ subcommand is not present in this port.
