![title]
Wind Generator

![brief]
Build a large generator from a stand base, 8–40 stand sections, a host computer, and a fan.

![content]
A Wind Generator consists of a __Wind Generator - Stand Base__, __Wind Generator - Stand__ sections, a __Wind Generator - Host Computer__, and a __Wind Generator - Fan__.

1. Place the stand base.
2. Stack 8–40 stand sections directly above it. The base does not count as a stand section.
3. Place the center of the host computer directly above the highest stand section.
4. Open the host computer and insert a Wind Generator Fan into its fan slot.

The 15×15 vertical plane directly in front of the host computer must be clear so the fan can turn. Any obstruction in that area stops generation.

Output depends on the host computer's altitude. It produces 7.5 IF/t at Y70 or below, increases gradually with altitude, and reaches 15 IF/t at Y160 or above.

The stand base stores up to 20,000 IF. Its charge slot can charge compatible items, and its energy can be supplied to connected equipment or a wireless node. Check the base and host-computer panels if the structure is incomplete or generation has stopped.
