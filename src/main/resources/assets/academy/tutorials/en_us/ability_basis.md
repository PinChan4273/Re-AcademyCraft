# Developing Your Ability

Open an __Ability Developer__ or the __Skill Tree__ app in the Data Terminal to review your ability level, learned skills, proficiency, and the requirements for skills you have not learned yet.

![Skill Tree](academy:textures/tutorial/skill_tree_ui.png)

## Learning Skills

Skills you have not learned are shown in grey. Your first skill has no skill prerequisite, but later skills may require a particular ability level, a higher-tier Ability Developer, or enough proficiency in another skill. Learning a skill consumes IF from the developer.

## Skill Proficiency

Using a skill raises its proficiency. Higher proficiency can improve the skill and may unlock additional ways to use it.

## Raising Your Ability Level

Using your ability increases your maximum CP. When your maximum CP reaches the threshold for the next level, use an Ability Developer to advance. Higher levels require more IF and may require a higher-tier developer.

## Resetting Your Ability

Resetting requires an __Advanced Ability Developer__ and an existing ability of level 3 or higher. Hold a __High-Voltage Magnetic Amplification Coil__ in your main hand and carry an __Induction Factor__ for a different ability category in your main inventory, then select reset after the developer finishes loading.

Completing the reset changes your ability category, lowers your ability by one level, clears all learned skills, consumes the coil, and consumes the entire stack of Induction Factors. Check the displayed requirements before you begin.

# Using Your Ability

## Basics

Use the __Ability Activation__ key ![key id="ability_activation"] to enter Ability Mode.

![Ability Mode](academy:textures/tutorial/ability_ui.png)

The __CP Bar__ in the upper-right shows your remaining Calculation Points and overload. The __Key Hint Bar__ on the right shows the current skill bindings.

## Presets

The Preset System lets you assign learned skills to keys. You can maintain four presets.

Use ![key id="edit_preset"] to open the preset editor.

![Preset Edit UI](academy:textures/tutorial/preset_selection_ui.png)

While Ability Mode is active, use ![key id="switch_preset"] to switch presets. Ability-related key bindings can be changed in the __Settings__ app.

## CP and Overload

Skills consume CP. CP recovers over time, so repeated use will eventually force you to rest.

Skills also produce short-term __Overload__, shown behind the CP bar. If overload reaches its limit, you cannot use skills again until it has recovered sufficiently.
