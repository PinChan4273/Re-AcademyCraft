![title]
Solar Generator

![brief]
Generate IF during the day when the panel has a clear view of the sky.

![content]
The Solar Generator produces __3 IF per tick__ in clear daytime weather. Rain reduces production to __0.6 IF per tick__. It does not generate at night or when the block above it cannot see the sky.

Its internal buffer holds 1,000 IF. Stored energy can still charge an item after generation stops, including at night or under a roof.

A single Solar Generator has limited output, so larger installations may need several generators or another source of IF.
