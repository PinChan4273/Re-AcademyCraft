![title]
Data Terminal

![brief]
Open your personal terminal and install additional apps with App Installers.

![content]
The Data Terminal includes three built-in apps: __MisakaCloud__, __Settings__, and __About__. Three optional apps—__Skill Tree__, __Media Player__, and __Frequency Transmitter__—are installed with their corresponding App Installers.

Use ![key id="open_data_terminal"] to open or close the Data Terminal.

__MisakaCloud__: read the Tutorial articles you have unlocked.

__Settings__: change key bindings and other client options.

__About__: view project and credit information.

__Skill Tree__: review your ability level, skill proficiency, and learnable skills.

__Frequency Transmitter__: connect generators and other wireless users to Nodes and Matrix networks.

__Media Player__: play user-supplied music. Commercial songs and cover artwork are not bundled with this port.
