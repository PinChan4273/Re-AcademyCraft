![title]
Metal Former

![brief]
Process materials in one of four modes to make plates, components, or additional resources.

![content]
The Metal Former has four modes: __Plate__, __Incise__, __Etch__, and __Refine__. Use the left and right arrows to select a mode.

__Plate__: turn a Constraint Metal Ingot into a Constraint Metal Plate, an Iron Ingot into a Reinforced Iron Plate, or two Reinforced Iron Plates into three Coins.

__Incise__: cut one Imag Silicon Ingot into two Wafers, or one Wafer into four Imag Silicon Pieces.

__Etch__: turn one Data Chip into one Calculation Chip.

__Refine__: process supported ore blocks into a larger material output. Vanilla ores and several Forge ore tags are supported; optional recipes are only loaded when the matching tags exist.

Changing modes immediately cancels the current processing progress. The input remains, but IF already spent is not refunded, so wait for the current operation to finish before changing modes.
