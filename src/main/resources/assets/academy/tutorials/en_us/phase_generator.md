![title]
Phase Generator

![brief]
Convert Imag Phase Liquid into IF and charge energy-storage items.

![content]
Insert filled Matter Units to transfer Imag Phase Liquid into the generator. Each unit supplies __1,000 mB__ and is returned as an empty Matter Unit.

The generator converts __1,000 mB__ into __500 IF__. Its tank holds 8,000 mB and its internal energy buffer holds 6,000 IF.

Place an item that can receive energy in the charging slot. Generation pauses when the internal buffer is full, and new Matter Units cannot be accepted if the returned-unit slot is blocked.
