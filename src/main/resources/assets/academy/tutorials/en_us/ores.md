Four AcademyCraft ores generate in the Overworld, and Imag Phase Liquid can be carried in Matter Units.

![content]
AcademyCraft adds __Constraint Metal Ore__, __Imag Silicon Ore__, __Imag Crystal Ore__, and __Resonant Crystal Ore__. With the default server configuration, all four generate in Overworld stone from Y=0 through Y=59. Changing `generic.genOres` affects new generation only; it does not remove ores from existing chunks.

# Constraint Metal Ore

Constraint Metal Ore requires a Stone Pickaxe or better. It drops itself and can be smelted into a Constraint Metal Ingot. Constraint Metal Plates are used in energy storage, machines, and other components.

# Imag Silicon Ore

Imag Silicon Ore requires an Iron Pickaxe or better and drops itself. Smelt it into an Imag Silicon Ingot, then process the ingot into Wafers and Imag Silicon Pieces for electronic components.

# Imag Crystal Ore and Resonant Crystal Ore

Both crystal ores require an Iron Pickaxe or better.

Imag Crystal Ore normally drops one or two Low-Purity Imag Crystals. Refine them in an Imag Fusor to obtain higher-purity crystals.

Resonant Crystal Ore drops a Resonant Crystal. Resonant Crystals are used in frequency-sensitive components for the wireless-energy system.

# Imag Phase Liquid

An empty Matter Unit can collect one source block of Imag Phase Liquid and becomes a filled Matter Unit. A filled unit places the source block again. Flowing liquid cannot be collected.

Each filled unit contains 1,000 mB. Imag Phase Liquid is used as fuel in the Phase Generator and as processing liquid in the Imag Fusor.
