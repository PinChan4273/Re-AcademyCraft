![title]
Ability Developer

![brief]
Acquire an ability, learn skills, raise your ability level, or reset your ability category.

![content]
Ability Developers consume IF while development is in progress.

There are three tiers: __Portable__, __Normal__, and __Advanced__. The Portable Ability Developer is used while held; Normal and Advanced developers must be placed. Higher-tier developers complete development faster and use less total IF.

Portable developers support skills up to level 2, Normal developers up to level 3, and Advanced developers up to level 5. Individual skills may also require a certain ability level, developer tier, or proficiency in prerequisite skills.

When you acquire your first ability, an Induction Factor gives you its category; without one, you get a category drawn evenly from the eligible categories. Factors are looked for in your main hand, your off hand, the rest of the hotbar (from the left), then the inventory (from the top left). Check the category and factor shown in the developer screen before you begin.

An Advanced Ability Developer can reset an existing ability category when all reset conditions are met: the current ability must be at least level 3 and a High-Voltage Magnetic Amplification Coil must be held in the main hand. An Induction Factor for a different category changes you to that category; without one, you change at random to a category other than your current one. Completing the reset lowers the ability by one level, clears the learned skills and consumes one High-Voltage Magnetic Amplification Coil (and one Induction Factor when a factor is used).

Closing the developer cancels active development. IF already spent is not refunded, so check the requirements and available energy before starting.
