![title]
Ability Developer

![brief]
Acquire an ability, learn skills, raise your ability level, or reset your ability category.

![content]
Ability Developers consume IF while development is in progress.

There are three tiers: __Portable__, __Normal__, and __Advanced__. The Portable Ability Developer is used while held; Normal and Advanced developers must be placed. Higher-tier developers complete development faster and use less total IF.

Portable developers support skills up to level 2, Normal developers up to level 3, and Advanced developers up to level 5. Individual skills may also require a certain ability level, developer tier, or proficiency in prerequisite skills.

To acquire your first ability, carry a registered Induction Factor for the desired category in your main inventory. Follow the requirements and status shown in the developer screen.

An Advanced Ability Developer can reset an existing ability category when all reset conditions are met: the current ability must be at least level 3, a High-Voltage Magnetic Amplification Coil must be held in the main hand, and an Induction Factor for a different category must be in the main inventory. Completing the reset lowers the ability by one level, clears the learned skills, consumes the coil, and consumes the entire factor stack.

Closing the developer cancels active development. IF already spent is not refunded, so check the requirements and available energy before starting.
