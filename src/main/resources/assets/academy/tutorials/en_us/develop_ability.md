Developing an Ability

![brief]
Prepare an Ability Developer and a reliable supply of Imaginary Energy (IF).

![content]
Ability Developers let you acquire an ability, learn skills, raise your ability level, and—under stricter conditions—reset your ability category.

There are three tiers: __Portable__, __Normal__, and __Advanced__. Portable developers support skills up to level 2, Normal developers up to level 3, and Advanced developers up to level 5. Higher tiers also develop skills faster and use less total IF.

Development consumes IF. The port currently provides __Wind__, __Solar__, and __Phase Generators__, as well as charged energy-storage items and the wireless network. See the corresponding articles for operating details.

Before starting, make sure the developer has enough energy and that you meet every requirement shown on its screen. Closing the developer cancels active development, and IF already spent is not refunded.

Develop your first ability, learn its skills, and keep practising to raise your level.
