Energy Bridge

![brief]
Legacy feature: Energy Bridge blocks are not currently available in this port.

![content]
The original Energy Bridge converted between Imaginary Energy (IF) and other energy systems. This legacy article is retained for source compatibility, but it is not registered in the current Tutorial app and no Energy Bridge block is implemented in the current port.

Do not plan a power network around the old conversion rates. Use the generators, energy-storage items, and wireless-network components that are present in this version.
